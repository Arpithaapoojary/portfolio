VedArogya

AI-Powered Ayurvedic Health Monitoring & Predictive Care System

Government Funded

VedArogya bridges classical Ayurvedic wisdom with modern digital health technology through a mobile application. It integrates Prakriti-based dosha assessment (Vata, Pitta, Kapha), real-time biometric data from wearables, and machine learning models to deliver personalized preventive healthcare rooted in Ayurvedic principles.

The platform is built on React Native (Expo) frontend, NestJS backend, FastAPI-based ML inference service, and a RAG knowledge pipeline trained on classical Ayurvedic texts including the Charaka Samhita, Ashtanga Hridayam, and Sushruta Samhita. A proprietary Agni Score replaces conventional calorie-based metrics to quantify digestive health.

Key features: Prakriti/Vikruti profiling, daily Dinacharya tracking, Ritucharya-based seasonal recommendations, dosha-biometric fusion analysis, and an AI Vaidya chatbot. Supports wearable integration via Google Health Connect and Apple HealthKit. Secured under ABDM/ABHA integration, DPDP Act 2023 compliance, and DISHA alignment. Clinically validated in partnership with Kakunje Wellness Ayurvedha Clinic, Moodbidri.

React Native NestJS FastAPI RAG Pipeline Machine Learning Google Health Connect Apple HealthKit Ayurveda AI ABDM/ABHA KSCST Funded
